1. China credit pulse: TSF and new loans vs. consensus → the impulse direction. 2. China property: weekly sales, starts, developer funding — the demand anchor. 3. The fix: PBoC daily USDCNY set vs. model estimate — management intent. 4. Trade cycle: Korea 20-day exports as the bellwether → global goods read. 5. Geopolitics: strait tensions, tech-export actions — transmission channel named. 8. Expression: FX, curve (when LIVE), or ETF — least-priced. 9. Benchmark divergence stated.

- **PBoC fix** — daily. Threshold: >100 pips vs. model estimate, sustained.
- **Korea 20-day exports** — the 21st, monthly. Threshold: ±5pp swing in the y/y.
