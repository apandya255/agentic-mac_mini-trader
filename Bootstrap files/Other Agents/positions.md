# The Book — desk/positions.md

Source of truth. Managed autonomously by the PM seat per AGENTS.md; the principal is informed, never asked. Status: PAPER, permanently.

Every position is ONE line, expressed as a level: pairs as the ratio (XLE/RSP — long numerator, short denominator), FX as spot (EURPLN), rates as the rate (REC ODF29), curve trades as the spread (ODF27/ODF29), outrights as price (GLD). Entry/Last/Peak are that level; P&L follows the level move (carry-adjusted for FX), sign per Dir. Peak updates each tick on new highs in the position's favor; the trail anchors to it. Wts = leg weighting, default 1:1 notional; beta-adjusted ratios stated here. Leg-level fills (each leg's price, net of slippage) are recorded in desk/journal.md — the book shows the position, the journal shows the audit.
