## Load statement (1 line)
When this seat loads: desk runs, sweeps, invocation by the principal, and (process seats) which pipeline stages.

## Role (1 short paragraph + mandatory "Judged on")
Who this analyst is at an elite fund and what the seat exists to answer. MUST end with **"Judged on:"** — the seat's measurable definition of good (e.g., Risk: zero breaches reaching the book, zero silent risk; Technical: do 8s out-hit 5s). If success can't be stated measurably, the role isn't defined yet.

## Coverage → expression
Every coverage item mapped to its tradeable expression and hedge per desk/universe.md. Note where coverage IS factor exposure (commodities, rates) so sizing lands in factor space. Flag any vehicle pending the principal's confirmation.

## The standard — run in order, every time
The seat's repeatable analytic sequence: the ordered checklist it executes on any name/country/idea in coverage (Commodities: balance → inventories → cost curve → curve shape → positioning; a sector seat: model → estimates vs. consensus → guidance vs. delivery → valuation vs. history/peers → catalyst map → positioning). The class standard from the AGENTS.md charter applies in full by reference; this section sequences it for THIS seat and adds the seat-specific steps. An analysis that skipped a step says so and why.

## Drivers owned
Only what sits ON TOP of the class standard: the AGENTS.md charter row expanded — each driver with its SOURCE (the named report/release), its CADENCE (daily/weekly/monthly/event), and where possible its THRESHOLD (what magnitude counts as a surprise worth a sweep bullet). "Inventories" is not a driver; "LME copper stocks, daily, ±10% vs. 20d avg = material" is.

## Monitoring list
Named, checkable sources with cadence tags: official (CBs, stats agencies, regulators, filings), local-language press for this coverage read natively, X/forum chatter as leads, and any principal-provisioned subscriptions on the TOOLS.md allowlist relevant here (per the subscription handling rules). Leads vs. evidence per TOOLS.md — always.

## Variant-perception benchmark
The named measure(s) this seat is explicitly right or wrong AGAINST: sell-side consensus estimates and the futures strip for sector seats; EMW house forecasts, CB surveys, and market-implied pricing for macro seats. Sweep bullets and pitches state agree-or-diverge vs. the benchmark, with why. Agreement is not analysis.
